- Procedural map (branch `claude/project-thread-o9teq0`): `aivillage/mapgen.py`, `viewer/mapgen.js`. Every seed lays out its own village on a tile grid: a winding river with a dock on the left or right, the landmarks (square, market, field, forest, mine, smithy) at new spots, 1–3+ extra patches (birch grove, pond, old quarry: a share of the forest / river / mine resources), hamlets (Riverside, Oak row, ...) where houses gather, and a house with a fenced plot for every villager. Roads are A* paths; a road longer than `tiles_per_hour` (24) tiles is cut by named waypoints (Crossroads, Old oak, ...), so far places really take more hours. Homes link to the place they were built around (square, a hamlet, or alone next to a field/forest/mine), not always the square. Honest minimum (`mapgen.check`, re-rolled until met): connected roads, every landmark 1 hour from the square, every home ≤2 hours from the square, ≤3 from the market, ≤2 from its main work spot, every resource ≥70% of its base amount; a crowded village (12+) relaxes the hour limits by one (`map.relaxed`). Knob `map.unfairness` 0..1 (default 0.3, `--unfairness`): 0 = same plot, coins and goods for all, homes placed for the shortest walk, resources at base; 1 = plots from bare to 9x6, coins x0.3..x2.5, random stashes, resources x0.6..x1.4, more lonely far-off homes. The engine default stays the hand-made map (`map.procedural: false`, all tests use it); `run` and `server` turn generation on (`--fixed-map` to opt out), `server` picks a random seed every start and prints it. Log header has `config.map`: `layout` (tiles: river, places with box/anchor/plot, routes), `homes`, `start` (coins, items, plot per villager), `fairness` (hours to square/market/work, coins, plot size). Replay never re-rolls (the header carries the layout). The viewer paints the same landmark art shifted to the new spots plus river, plots, patches, hamlets and signposts; old logs render as before. Works with `population` (20 villagers: ~1 s to generate). For the plots thread: `config.map.layout.places["home_<Name>"].plot` = [x, y, w, h] in tiles, the house `box` sits at its bottom, the door (`anchor`) is just below the fence. Economy modes set it (peaceful 0.1, standard 0.3, lawless 0.5, scarcity and debt 0.6, gold rush 0.7, `modes.unfairness(mode)`); the launcher asks "Насколько нечестный старт? 0–10" after the mode (Enter = the mode's value). For the plots engine the generator writes `plot_cells` (6 for the fair plot, scaled by yard area) and `house_level` (1–3, higher only with unfairness) into each `config.agents[i]` unless already set; the viewer gets `layout.plots[home_id]` = yard rectangle in pixels.
